# Week 17 — Apr 27

## AI Learning

TabFM, Google Research's zero-shot foundation model for tabular data: applies in-context learning (the training table itself as "prompt") to classification/regression, trained entirely on synthetic structural-causal-model data, no per-dataset training or tuning required. Discussion pushed past the headline claim — the TabFM-Ensemble variant needing hand-engineered cross-features/SVD features on top, and the `max_num_rows=100` context cap, both point to the "no feature engineering required" pitch relocating the work (to context curation and retrieval) rather than eliminating it.

## Build

Part 2 of the Signal Protocol Chat (Part 1: [Week 15](week-15-2026-04-13.md)): took the local, single-device crypto simulation and turned it into a real, working end-to-end encrypted chat between two actual phones over the internet.

Added a Firestore-backed relay for pre-key bundles and ciphertext, replaced `InMemorySignalProtocolStore` with a persistent on-device store so identity/session state survives app restarts, and used Firebase Anonymous Auth to give each device a stable identity. Spent real time on the security model of introducing a live backend: why the Firebase API key isn't a secret and Firestore Security Rules are the actual access-control boundary, how a UID differs from the signed JWT that Firestore actually trusts, why knowing someone's UID doesn't let you impersonate them, and hardening `AndroidManifest.xml`'s `allowBackup` setting now that the app holds a real credential (the Auth refresh token) worth protecting.
