# Week 16 — Apr 20

## AI Learning

Studied the [Open Knowledge Format (OKF)](../tools/open-knowledge-format.md), Google's open spec for representing AI-agent context as markdown files with YAML frontmatter. It formalizes the "LLM-wiki" pattern — the same structure this repo's own `wiki/` implements — into a vendor-neutral, portable standard for knowledge bundles, with only `type` required per concept and optional provenance/trust/lifecycle metadata layered on top.

## Build

Part 2 of the Bike Sharing Feature Pipeline (Part 1: [Week 13](week-13-2026-03-30.md)): three more stages of feature engineering, built forward from Part 1's best-performing feature set (time-derived/cyclical features, RMSLE 0.7365) rather than its one-hot-encoded stage.

- **Numeric transforms & interactions**: ordinal binning of `temp`/`humidity`, plus two interaction terms (`temp_humidity`, `workingday_rush`). RMSLE = 0.6646 — the largest single jump in the whole pipeline. An ablation isolating bins vs. interactions showed the entire lift came from the interaction terms; the bins alone were statistically indistinguishable from not adding anything.
- **Feature selection**: pruned 9 low-importance columns (using the trained model's own `feature_importances_`) down to 12. RMSLE = 0.6647 — tied with the full feature set, but with a simpler model. Notably, `temp_humidity` — one of the two interaction terms — was among the features pruned: despite being a plausible domain interaction (heat + humidity = discomfort), its correlation with the actual target was only 0.07, far weaker than `temp` or `feel_temp` alone, because `temp` and `humidity` pull in opposite directions on ridership and their product muddles rather than amplifies either signal.
- **Summary**: consolidated comparison across all 5 real stages (Part 1 + Part 2), confirming stage 4 (interactions) as the highest-leverage change in the entire pipeline, ahead of both the time-feature and categorical-encoding work.
