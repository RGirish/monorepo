# Week 7 — Feb 16

## AI Learning: Embedding Models and Vector Similarity

Embedding models convert text (or other data) into dense numerical vectors where semantic similarity is preserved as geometric proximity. This week covered how embedding models are trained and used, along with the three primary similarity algorithms: cosine similarity (measures the angle between vectors, scale-invariant), dot product (combines magnitude and direction), and Euclidean distance (straight-line distance in vector space). These are the foundational concepts behind semantic search, RAG pipelines, and recommendation systems.
