# Week 18 — May 04

## AI Learning: Prompt Engineering

Worked through prompt engineering best practices interactively, sourced from Anthropic's own 2026 prompt engineering guide and an Educative overview. Covered being explicit/specific (including *why* explaining a constraint's reasoning helps the model generalize correctly), few-shot examples (and how an example that contradicts a written instruction introduces run-to-run nondeterminism rather than being cleanly overridden), chain-of-thought (grounded in why autoregressive generation makes externalized reasoning steps actually change answer quality, not just readability), prefilling (working out precisely what "the model can't revise already-generated tokens" does and doesn't guarantee — a hard constraint on committed tokens, but still probabilistic sampling after that point), and prompt chaining (context isolation and per-step checkpoints, walked through with a three-step legal-memo example).

Also surveyed the broader technique landscape beyond these fundamentals — Tree/Graph of Thought, Self-Consistency, ReAct, Reflexion, APE, multi-agent debate — and worked out which of these can run as a single prompt versus which fundamentally require external orchestration (tool execution, independent sampling, or dynamic control flow outside the model's own generation).

## Build: Embedding Vector Quantization

Explored the embedding-compression landscape (scalar quantization, product quantization, binary quantization/hashing, dimensionality reduction, Matryoshka Representation Learning, and how they compose — see the [survey](../synthesis/embedding-compression-techniques.md)), then implemented int8 scalar quantization end-to-end: asymmetric quantization (min/max calibration with a zero-point shift) and symmetric quantization (zero-centered, no shift). Worked through why symmetric quantization lets similarity search run directly on compressed int8 values via a single scalar correction factor, rather than requiring dequantization back to floats first — verified numerically that top-10 nearest-neighbor rankings survive unchanged under both schemes at a 4x storage reduction.
